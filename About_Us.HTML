About Us


This website is dedicated to fish because the owner likes fish.

Our mission is to spread the love for fish

Fishes are aquatic animals whose general features are gills, fins and a slim, slippery body for efficient swimming.They come in a variety of shapes, sizes, and colors, living in oceans, rivers, lakes, and streams. They play a crucial role in aquatic ecosystems as both predators and prey, and many species are important for human consumption.
